# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shoreward LLC ("**Shoreward**", "**we**", "**us**") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information in connection with Horizon, our AI-assisted software delivery service (the "**Service**"). It applies to the Horizon web application and its related integrations.

Horizon is provided to businesses. If your organization has signed an agreement with us (an order form, master services agreement or data processing agreement), that agreement governs our processing of your organization's data and takes precedence over this policy where they conflict.

## 1. Our role

- **Account and usage information.** For information about the people who sign in to Horizon, and about how the Service is used, Shoreward acts as a **controller**.
- **Customer Content.** For the content a customer brings into Horizon — work items, repository code, issues, pull requests, plans, reviews and the outputs Horizon generates for them — Shoreward acts as a **processor** (or "service provider") on behalf of the customer. The customer decides what content is processed and is the controller of any personal information within it. Requests about Customer Content should be directed to the customer organization, and we will assist them as their agreement requires.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you provide**

- **Account details:** name, email address, and the sign-in method you use (email and password, or Google sign-in).
- **Security credentials:** a gate PIN, used to authorize approvals. We store it only as a salted one-way hash, never the PIN itself.
- **Customer Content:** anything you or your organization create or connect, including work-item titles, descriptions, success criteria, guardrails, comments, feedback and approval decisions.
- **Messaging details:** if your organization enables WhatsApp notifications or approvals, the phone number or WhatsApp identifier of each approver, and the content of the related messages.
- **Support communications:** the content of messages you send us.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Session data:** when you sign in we set a session cookie (see section 7). We store only a hash of the session token, with its creation and expiry times.
- **Activity records:** timestamps of sign-ins, and an audit trail of actions taken in the Service (such as approvals, rejections and pauses), attributed to the user who took them.
- **Technical logs:** IP address, browser and device information, request URLs and error details, recorded by our servers for security and reliability.

**Information from third parties**

- **Google:** if you choose Google sign-in, Google provides your name, email address and a stable account identifier.
- **GitHub and other connected tools:** when your organization connects a repository, we receive the issues, pull requests, labels, code and related metadata needed to deliver the Service, including the usernames of contributors.

## 3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

- provide, operate and maintain the Service, including running the AI agents that plan, implement and review work on your organization's behalf;
- authenticate users, authorize approvals and keep an accurate audit trail;
- send service notifications, such as alerts that an item is waiting for an approval;
- secure the Service, and detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
- troubleshoot, measure and improve the reliability and performance of the Service;
- communicate with you about your account, the Service and changes to our terms or policies; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

**We do not sell personal information**, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use Customer Content to train AI models, whether our own or third-party.

## 4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: **performance of a contract** (to provide the Service you or your organization signed up for); **legitimate interests** (to secure, maintain and improve the Service, and to keep audit records, balanced against your rights); **legal obligation** (where the law requires processing); and **consent**, where we ask for it, which you may withdraw at any time.

## 5. AI processing

Horizon uses third-party AI model providers to generate plans, code, reviews and other outputs from Customer Content. Content is sent to these providers only as needed to perform the requested task. They process it on our behalf under contractual terms that restrict them from using it to train their models. AI-generated outputs can be inaccurate, and a person at your organization reviews and approves them at defined gates before they take effect.

## 6. How we share information

We share personal information only as follows:

- **Subprocessors** that help us run the Service, under written contracts that require them to protect the information and use it only to provide services to us. These include cloud hosting (Amazon Web Services), AI model providers (such as Anthropic and, where enabled, Meta), authentication (Google), messaging (WhatsApp, operated by Meta, where enabled) and web fonts (Google Fonts). A current list is available on request.
- **Services your organization connects**, such as GitHub. We send data to them at your organization's direction, and their own terms and privacy policies govern how they handle it.
- **Within your organization:** other authorized users in your organization can see work items, activity and approvals attributed to you.
- **Legal and safety:** where required by law or legal process, or where needed to protect the rights, property or safety of Shoreward, our customers or others.
- **Business transfers:** in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, subject to the protections in this policy.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

Horizon uses **only strictly necessary storage**:

- a session cookie (secure, HTTP-only, valid for up to 30 days), which keeps you signed in; and
- your browser's local storage, to remember interface preferences such as light or dark theme.

We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies. Pages load web fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when it serves the fonts.

## 8. Data retention

- **Account information** is kept while your account is active, and deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period after it is closed.
- **Customer Content** is kept for the duration of your organization's agreement, then deleted or returned as that agreement specifies.
- **Sessions** expire after at most 30 days.
- **Technical logs** are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.

We may keep information longer where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

## 9. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. These include encryption in transit (TLS), one-way hashing of approval PINs and session tokens, access controls, and a separate approval credential for consequential actions. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected customers and individuals as the law requires.

## 10. International transfers

Shoreward is based in the United States, and we and our subprocessors may process information in the United States and other countries. Where we transfer personal information out of the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we use appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and their UK and Swiss equivalents.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate information;
- delete your information;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive your information in a portable format;
- withdraw consent, where we rely on it; and
- lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, contact us at the address below. We will verify your identity before acting on it. If your request concerns Customer Content, we will refer you to the customer organization, which controls that data, and help them respond.

**California residents.** Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion and correction; and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information as the CCPA defines those terms. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

## 12. Children

The Service is intended for business use, and not for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new effective date, and where the changes are material we will notify customers in advance by email or through the Service.
